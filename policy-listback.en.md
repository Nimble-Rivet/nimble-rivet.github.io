# Listback Privacy Policy

App publisher: **Nimble Rivet**.
Support and privacy contact: **nimblerivet@gmail.com**.
Last updated: **October 29, 2026**.

Listback is a reusable checklist app with run history. It processes the titles,
descriptions, sections, items, instructions, notes, checkmarks and run dates you
enter to save your checklists and display their history.

## Local data

Checklists and history are stored in the app's local database on your device.
Your chosen theme and color palette are stored separately on the same device.
Technical record identifiers connect records and are not user-tracking IDs.

Listback does not require an account and contains no advertising, built-in
analytics or crash reporting to the developer's servers. It makes no network
requests of its own and does not automatically send checklist contents to the developer.

## Backups and other apps

When you request it, Listback creates a JSON file containing all templates and
runs, including archived templates, instructions, statuses, notes and dates.
Appearance preferences are not included. You choose the destination in Android's
system file picker: device storage, an external drive or an installed document provider.

The selected provider receives the file. A cloud provider may transfer it to its
servers under its own terms and privacy policy. Listback does not control how
that provider stores, transmits or deletes the exported copy.

JSON files are plain text and are not encrypted by Listback. Anyone with access
to a file can read its contents. Choose a destination appropriate for the
information in your lists. Backups are manual and do not synchronize devices.

## Restoring data

Listback reads and validates the file you select, then previews its contents.
After your confirmation, the file's checklists and history replace the current
local database. Appearance preferences remain unchanged. Android grants access
to the document you choose; Listback does not request access to all device files.

## Retention and deletion

Data remains until you change or delete it. Individual runs can be deleted in
the app. Archiving a template keeps it rather than deleting it. Clearing Listback's
app data in Android settings or uninstalling the app removes its local database
and preferences. Exported backups remain in the destination you chose and must
be deleted separately.

## Data protection

The app uses Android's data isolation and does not add its own encryption to the
database or JSON files. Android Auto Backup is disabled; the app's rules exclude
its data from system cloud backup and device transfer. Use a verified manual
backup to move your checklists. System software and installed document providers
operate under their own rules.

## Contacting support

For questions about Listback or its data handling, write to **nimblerivet@gmail.com**.
When you send an email, your mail service transmits your sender address, message
and any files you attach. This is a separate action outside the app. Do not attach
a backup or personal details unless they are needed for your request.

## Policy changes

This policy will be updated when Listback's data handling changes, together with
the description of the affected features.
