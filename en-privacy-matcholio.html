What Matcholio stores

Matcholio keeps player names or nicknames, optional player photos, game names and rules, lineups, match dates and results, statistics, and preferences on your device. You can use nicknames and the built-in avatars instead of personal names and photos. A player can have a neutral, happy and sad photo. Photos are selected or taken by you; this version does not generate expressions using AI.

Local operation

No Matcholio account is required. This version has no developer-operated server, advertising, analytics or crash-reporting service, and does not upload your match history or photos itself. There is no automatic cloud backup or Google Drive synchronization. Android, your app store and the external apps you choose have their own data practices; this policy describes Matcholio.

Photos and camera

The system photo picker gives Matcholio access to the image you select, rather than your entire photo library. If you take a photo, a camera app receives temporary access to the capture file. That camera app operates under its own settings and privacy policy. Matcholio copies the chosen image into its private storage, corrects its orientation and converts it to PNG with a maximum edge of 1024 pixels. It does not copy the original EXIF metadata during this conversion. Images restored from a backup retain their PNG bytes and may contain metadata already present in those files. Matcholio does not identify people or perform face recognition.

Export and restore

Exporting creates an unencrypted ZIP file containing the game history, players, game rules and referenced player photos, including happy and sad photos. Archived players or games and cancelled matches are also included. Preferences and unreferenced photo files are not included. You choose the destination using the system document picker. If you choose a cloud document provider or later share the file, that provider or recipient receives it. Anyone who can open the ZIP can read its contents. Choose a destination and recipients you trust. Restore reads a file you select and, after confirmation, replaces the local game data; it does not merge results from multiple devices. Matcholio does not manage or delete your exported copies.

Optional spoken confirmation

If you enable voice confirmation, Matcholio sends only a fixed result-saved phrase to your selected system text-to-speech engine. It does not send player names, photos, scores or match details to that engine. Some speech engines or voices use online processing under their own policies. You can turn voice confirmation off in Settings.

Retention and deletion

Game records remain in the app until you replace them by restoring a backup, clear the app storage or uninstall it. Restoring game records does not guarantee removal of old photo files. Archiving a player or game and cancelling a match retain the underlying records; they are not permanent deletion. Removing or replacing a photo removes its active reference after you save, but an old photo file can remain in private storage. Discarding an unfinished edit can also leave an unused photo file. To remove all Matcholio data on this device, use Android Settings → Apps → Matcholio → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall Matcholio. Menu names vary by device. Delete exported ZIP files separately from each local or cloud destination and from any recipients. Android automatic app backup and device transfer are disabled for Matcholio data.

Protection and changes

Android app storage isolates Matcholio data from ordinary access by other apps. Matcholio does not add its own password or encryption to exported ZIP files. Protect your device and backups with the security controls available to you. This local policy is bundled with the app; an updated version of Matcholio may include a revised policy reflecting changed features or data practices.

Publisher and contact

Matcholio is published by Nimble Rivet. For support, privacy questions or requests about personal data, contact nimblerivet@gmail.com.
